Our Training Playbook

Two individual plans, one place to track each workout. Enter the weight and reps for every set, and use the history to see your progress.

How to use this plan

Your records

Entries save automatically on this browser and device. GitHub Pages does not sync logs between phones. Export a backup regularly; import it to move your records to another device.

Workout history

Saved sessions for the selected person. Choose one to reopen its log.

Start with loads that allow controlled form. An empty box means an unrecorded set; check the box when completed. kg is the total external weight used, or the weight of one dumbbell when the exercise says “each”.
